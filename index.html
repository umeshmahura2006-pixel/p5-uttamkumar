About the Department

A technology-focused academic environment designed to transform curiosity into innovation.

The Department of Computer Science provides quality education and develops technical and problem-solving skills among students. Our academic programs combine fundamental computer science concepts with emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cloud Computing and Computer Networks.
